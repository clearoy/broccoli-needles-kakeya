Two formal definitions

Hausdorff
$$\mathcal{H}^s(E)=\lim_{\delta\to0}\ \inf\Big\{\sum_i(\operatorname{diam}U_i)^s \;:\; E\subset\bigcup_i U_i,\ \operatorname{diam}U_i\lt\delta\Big\}$$ $$\dim_H E=\inf\{s\ge 0:\ \mathcal{H}^s(E)=0\}$$
Minkowski (box-counting)
$$N(\varepsilon)=\min\#\{\varepsilon\text{-boxes covering }E\}$$ $$\dim_M E=\lim_{\varepsilon\to0}\frac{\log N(\varepsilon)}{\log(1/\varepsilon)}$$
$$\dim_M E \;=\; n-\lim_{\varepsilon\to0}\frac{\log \big|E_\varepsilon\big|}{\log \varepsilon}$$
$$\dim_H E\;\le\;\underline{\dim}_M E\;\le\;\overline{\dim}_M E$$ equal for the Koch curve: $\log 4/\log 3$
