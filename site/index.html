Area → 0, but dimension = 2

$$m(x) = \#\{T : x\in T\},\qquad E=\bigcup_T T$$
$$\int m\,dx = \sum_T |T| \approx \delta^{-1}\cdot\delta = 1$$
$$\int m^2\,dx = \sum_{i,j}|T_i\cap T_j| \lesssim \sum_{i,j}\frac{\delta^2}{|\theta_i-\theta_j|+\delta} \approx \log\tfrac1\delta$$
$$1 \approx \Big(\int_E m\,dx\Big)^2 \le \int_E 1\cdot\int_E m^2\,dx \lesssim |E|\,\log\tfrac1\delta$$
$$|E| \gtrsim \frac{1}{\log(1/\delta)} \;\gg\; \delta^{\,2-d}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dim K = 2$$
